We are moving exceptionally quickly with AI across industries and countries. The pace of has reached unprecedented levels, and with it comes a reality that many organizations are just beginning to grapple with: the depreciation of software has never been greater.

What was cutting-edge just months ago is now legacy. Tools and platforms that required significant investment are rapidly becoming obsolete. This isn't just about keeping up with the latest trends—it's about survival in an increasingly complex landscape. 

This introduce tremendous employee fatigure as both junior and senior staff adapter to new platforms and concepts, only for them to be surplanted months later. 

Organizations are going to be challenged over the next 12-24 months with high costs and low returns. The traditional approach of investing in technology solutions with the expectation of long-term value is being upended. The shelf life of technical decisions is shrinking, and the financial implications are profound.

The question isn't whether this rapid change will continue—it will - for some time atleast. The question is: how will organizations and individuals adapt when the very foundations they're building on shift beneath them?
